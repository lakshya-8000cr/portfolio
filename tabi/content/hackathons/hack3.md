Won **Best First Year Innovator** award for building a real-time collaborative workspace for university students.

## Problem

Students struggle with fragmented study workflows—switching between separate apps for document sharing, live group discussions, and notes during exam sprints.

## Solution

EduSphere provides a single unified web platform with low-latency real-time document sync, group study rooms, and quick resource sharing.

## Key engineering decisions & Chaos

- Utilized WebSockets for instant state synchronization across multiple concurrent client sessions.
- Focused on minimal frontend overhead so the app loaded seamlessly on low-bandwidth college Wi-Fi.
- **The Hackathon Vibe:** Racing against the clock to fix dynamic room-joining race conditions with 20 minutes left on the submission timer.

## Technology stack

- **Frontend:** React, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Node.js, Socket.io, Express
- **Database:** MongoDB

## Outcome & Learnings

 Awarded **Best First Year Innovator**. Proved that delivering a polished, zero-crash core feature is 10x better than presenting a feature-bloated MVP to judges.
