Competed at **HackMol 7.0**, a 36-hour offline hackathon hosted at Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology (NIT), Jalandhar. 

Out of **900+ registered teams**, our team **DOMinators** cleared the initial screening into the Top 60 offline finalists and secured **4th Rank Overall** 🏆.

## Problem

Building a complex end-to-end MVP within 36 hours that satisfies technical architecture checks, real-world utility, and tight demo evaluations.

## Solution

Designed and deployed a high-performance application featuring a decoupled backend, live client interactions, and efficient API execution under strict speed constraints.

## Key engineering decisions & Chaos

- **Precision vs Speed:** Missed 3rd place by a razor-thin **1.98 points**—reinforcing how every micro-optimization in presentation and feature completeness counts.
- **Double Win:** Got shortlisted among the **Top 10 candidates** for an internship role by event sponsor **HelloRecruiter.ai** based on resume and project profile evaluation.
- **The Hackathon Vibe:** 36 hours of continuous coding with team **DOMinators** (Aditya, Vaibhav, and me), navigating 3 AM architectural blockers, zero sleep, and pure dev energy.

## Technology stack

- **Backend & APIs:** Node.js / Express micro-architecture
- **Frontend & Real-Time:** React, WebSockets / Dynamic UI
- **Infrastructure:** Docker containerized deployment setup

## Outcome & Learnings

- **4th Rank Overall** out of 250+ teams.
- **Top 10 Shortlist** for HelloRecruiter.ai internship.
- Taught us how to pitch complex backend workflows cleanly under intense judge scrutiny and proved how close we are to taking 1st place in the next sprint.
